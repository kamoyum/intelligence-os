# Code of Conduct

Intelligence OS is an experimental safety- and evidence-oriented project. Participation should help improve the quality of the system and the quality of the reasoning around it.

## Expected behavior

- be respectful and specific
- critique ideas and implementations, not people
- distinguish evidence, inference, and opinion
- disclose uncertainty
- welcome counterexamples and failed hypotheses
- protect private and sensitive information
- do not pressure contributors to ship unsafe authority expansion for speed

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment or personal attacks
- publishing another person's sensitive data
- posting credentials or exploit details that create avoidable risk
- intentionally bypassing project safety boundaries in shared environments
- presenting unverified claims as established project facts

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project.
